# Assets and original work

`assets/office-cinematic.webp`: original Boring Office office backdrop, generated 2026-10-04 using the built-in imagegen tool. Prompt: original cinematic modern office, straight-on view from employee desk, rainy city, teal-grey furniture and warm practical light, clear central negative space; no people, typography, logos, UI, weapons or borrowed game assets. Resized to 1440 px maximum width, WebP quality 82. Prompt and provenance are retained here; generated artwork is not represented as a third-party CC0 asset.

Office enemies, room geometry, retro background, boss illustrations, VFX and original procedural chiptune are project-created Canvas/Web Audio assets. No Nintendo, Capcom, SNK or other commercial-game assets were used.

Third-party music: see [assets/MUSIC-CREDITS.md](assets/MUSIC-CREDITS.md), which records author, exact licence, source, dates and modifications. Existing player/tank and supplied original audio are retained with existing provenance; no new licensing claims are made for those legacy files.
